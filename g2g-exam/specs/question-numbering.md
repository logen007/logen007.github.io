# Whole-exam question numbering

APPROVED by user, 13 October 2026. Overrides older per-skill numbering fields.

Scored questions are numbered consecutively across all sections of the exam.
Examples/prefilled questions do not consume numbers. Use this sequence for editor,
question labels, validation errors, preview/runtime and review/grading.
Single-question writing may hide its label, but still consumes its number.
Do not rewrite authored question text or internal question IDs/codes.
The shared implementation is src/domain/question-numbering.js.
