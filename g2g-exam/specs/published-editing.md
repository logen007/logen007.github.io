# Published exam editing — APPROVED

User approved 2026-10-10. Supersedes publication/attempt structure locks.
- Exam owners and master admins can edit published exams, including structure, after attempts exist.
- Other teachers cannot modify another teacher's exam.
- Original shared/locked questions are forked when opening an owned exam.
- Publishing does not re-lock the editor.
- Existing attempts retain their original exam and question snapshot. Legacy attempts receive a snapshot before authoring changes.
- Uploaded media referenced by snapshots remains protected from garbage collection.
